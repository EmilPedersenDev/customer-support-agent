# Test Customer – Company Overview

## About the Company

Test Customer is a defense technology company specializing in secure mission systems, advanced surveillance platforms, and integrated command software. The company develops solutions used in high-security environments where reliability, resilience, and information security are critical.

Its systems are designed for government agencies, defense organizations, and authorized security partners operating in both domestic and international contexts.

## Founded

Test Customer was founded in 2008 with the goal of building next-generation secure communication and situational awareness systems for defense operations.

Since its founding, the company has expanded from a small engineering-focused team into a multi-domain technology provider with expertise in software, hardware integration, and data-driven defense systems.

## Headquarters and Locations

The company is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, with additional engineering and support offices in:

- Gothenburg (software development and AI systems)
- Linköping (hardware and sensor integration)
- Oslo (regional support and deployment coordination)

## Core Business Areas

Test Customer operates across four main product domains:

### Command & Control Systems

Real-time operational platforms for mission coordination and decision support.

### Secure Communications

Encrypted communication systems for field units and command centers.

### Surveillance & Sensor Systems

Multi-source data fusion platforms combining drones, radar, and satellite data.

### Intelligence Analysis

Software tools for processing operational data into actionable intelligence.

## Customers

The company primarily serves:

- National defense organizations
- Border security agencies
- Emergency response and disaster coordination units
- Allied defense contractors and integrators

## Mission

To provide secure, reliable, and intelligent systems that support critical operations in complex and high-risk environments.

## Technology Focus

Test Customer invests heavily in:

- Real-time distributed systems
- Secure-by-design architecture
- Edge computing for disconnected environments
- AI-assisted intelligence analysis
- Resilient communications under degraded network conditions

## Compliance and Standards

All systems are developed in compliance with relevant defense and security standards, including export control regulations and secure software development practices.

## Summary

Test Customer delivers integrated defense technology solutions that combine communication, surveillance, and intelligence capabilities into unified operational platforms used in mission-critical environments.
